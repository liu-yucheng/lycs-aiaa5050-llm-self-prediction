Why I built this我为什么做这个实验

The course asks a hard question: does a large language model have anything like a self? I cannot answer that question with a definition. I can measure one narrow, checkable part of it. If a model holds a distribution over the next token, and if it can also report that distribution, then the model carries a usable model of its own computation. If it cannot, then the phrase the model knows itself needs a narrower meaning.课程提出一个很难的问题：大模型有没有类似自我的东西？我不能用定义回答这个问题，但可以测量其中一个狭窄且可检验的部分。如果模型持有下一个词块的分布，并且也能报告这个分布，那么模型就带有自己计算过程的可用的模型。如果做不到，那么模型认识自己这句话就需要更窄的含义。

The gap between the two is the measurement. That is the whole experiment: no introspection claim, no consciousness claim, one number with an interval.两者之间的差距就是测量结果。整个实验只有这个：不主张内省，不主张意识，只给出一个带区间的数字。

Course topics课程主题

The course module Understanding LLMs builds the pipeline of a decoder: tokenize, embed, attend, feed forward, decode one token at a time. Next-token prediction is the loop of that pipeline. This experiment reads the output of the loop in two ways, once from the interface and once from the model as text.课程模块《理解大模型》讲清解码器的流程：分词、嵌入、注意力、前馈、逐个解码。下一个词块预测就是这个流程的循环。本实验用两种方式读取该循环的输出：一次从接口读，一次让模型用文字写出。

The module Seventy Years at a Glance sorts the literature by question. The third question is does it know itself. This experiment turns that question into a protocol with four conditions, three repetitions, and one divergence.模块《研究全景》按问题给文献分类。第三个问题是它认得自己吗。本实验把这个问题变成一个协议：四个条件、三次重复、一个散度。

The module Are LLMs Conscious separates the whole question into parts: the whole picture, the self, the stake, and measuring it. This experiment belongs to the last part. It measures a capability, and it states no position on experience.模块《大模型有意识吗》把整个问题拆成几部分：整体图景、自我、利害、一起去量。本实验属于最后一部分。它测量一项能力，不对体验表态。

The modules on consciousness theory and on the Turing test give two habits that this experiment keeps. First, a claim about a machine needs an operational test. Second, a test measures the test and not the thing behind it.意识理论与图灵测试两个模块给出两个习惯，本实验都保留。第一，关于机器的判断需要可操作的检验。第二，检验测量的是检验本身，不是它背后的东西。

Debate topics辩论主题

The debate class runs two rounds, one technical and one beyond technology, and every motion asks for a position that a measurement can inform but cannot settle. Three motions sit close to this experiment.辩论课有两场，一场技术向，一场非技术向。每道辩题都要求一个立场，测量可以支持这个立场，但不能决定它。三道辩题与本实验接近。

  • Motion: a model that reports its own distribution understands itself. The experiment supplies the first half of the premise and refuses the second half.辩题：能报告自己分布的模型理解自己。实验提供前提的前半部分，不对后半部分表态。
  • Motion: self-report is evidence of inner life. The experiment shows that self-report can be measured and can be wrong, so the motion needs a further step.辩题：自我报告是内在生命的证据。实验表明自我报告可以被测量，也可能出错，因此该辩题还需要一步论证。
  • Motion: we should trust a model about its own computation more than we trust its output. The divergence between the two is the quantity that the motion needs.辩题：关于自己的计算，我们应该更相信模型的说明，而不是它的输出。两者之间的散度正是该辩题需要的量。
