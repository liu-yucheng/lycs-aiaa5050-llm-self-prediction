Meaning and Limits意义与局限

What these numbers show, what they do not show, and what to measure next.这些数字说明什么，不说明什么，下一步测什么。

What a small divergence means小散度说明什么

A small divergence in the plain condition means that the text table of the model matches the served table at the resolution of the measurement. That is a statement about a capability: the model can report its own distribution. It is not a statement about experience, and it is not a statement about access to the computation that produced the distribution.普通条件下的小散度表示：在本次测量的分辨率下，模型写出的文字表格与接口表格一致。这是关于能力的一句话：模型能报告自己的分布。它不是关于体验的论断，也不是关于能访问产生该分布的计算过程的论断。

What a large divergence means大散度说明什么

A large divergence in the plain condition means that the model cannot state its own distribution. The model can still produce the distribution, because the interface reads it at each step. The gap between producing and reporting is the measured quantity.普通条件下的大散度表示：模型无法说出自己的分布。模型仍然能产生该分布，因为接口在每一步读取它。产生与报告之间的差距就是被测量的量。

The injection comparisons注入比较

The injected self condition controls for the mechanics of the task. If the model copies the served table with little error, then a large divergence in the plain condition comes from the model and not from the difficulty of writing a table.注入自我条件控制了任务的机械难度。如果模型几乎无误差地复制接口表格，那么普通条件下的大散度来自模型本身，而不是来自写表格的难度。

The injected actual condition tests influence in the other direction. A small divergence means that the model ignores the self-predicted table when it predicts. A large divergence means that the table moves the prediction.注入实际条件检验相反方向的影响。小散度表示模型在预测时忽略了自我预测表格。大散度表示该表格改变了预测。

Limits局限

  • The measurement covers one token at one position. It says nothing about a long reply.测量只覆盖一个位置的一个词块。它不涉及长回复。
  • The table holds the top entries only. A model that reports the top entries well can still hold a different tail.表格只保存前若干条目。报告好前若干条目的模型，尾部仍然可能不同。
  • The tokenizer is invisible to the model. A word that the tokenizer cuts into pieces limits the exact match of the top token.分词器对模型不可见。被切成片段的词会限制首位词块的精确一致。
  • The floor of 1e-12 exaggerates one missing token. The report shows the coverage so that a reader can see this effect.1e-12 的概率下限会放大单个缺失词块的影响。报告给出覆盖率，读者可以看出这一影响。
  • A provider can serve a quantized or a routed variant of a model. The recorded model name is the name that the provider reports.提供方可能提供量化版本或路由版本。记录的模型名是提供方报告的模型名。
  • The repetition count is small. The interval covers the sampling of tasks and repetitions in this run, and no other source of variation.重复次数较少。区间只覆盖本次运行的任务抽样与重复抽样，不覆盖其他变异来源。

Future work未来工作

  • Measure a whole reply, one position at a time, and compare the error along it.逐个位置测量一整段回复，并比较误差沿回复的变化。
  • Ask for the tail as well as the head, with a second prompt for rare tokens.除头部外也询问尾部，用第二个提示词覆盖稀有词块。
  • Vary the temperature and the reasoning setting, and report each cell.改变温度与推理开关，逐格报告结果。
  • Give the model its own served table and ask it to explain the difference from its self-report.把接口表格给模型，让它解释与自我报告的差异。
